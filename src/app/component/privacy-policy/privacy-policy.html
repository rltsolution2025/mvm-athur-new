Privacy Policy

Maharishi Vidya Mandir School is committed to protecting the privacy and security of students, parents, guardians, staff members, and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including student details, parent/guardian information, contact details, academic records, admission information, and communication preferences through admission forms, enquiry forms, and school interactions.

2. Purpose of Information Collection

Information is collected to process admissions, maintain student records, provide educational services, communicate important school updates, manage academic activities, and improve school operations.

3. Student & Parent Information

Personal details submitted by parents and guardians are used strictly for educational and administrative purposes. Information is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel.

4. Communication Consent

By submitting admission or enquiry forms, parents and guardians consent to receive communication from the school through phone calls, SMS, WhatsApp, email, or other official channels regarding admissions, academics, fee reminders, events, and important updates.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Sharing of Information

Maharishi Vidya Mandir does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be shared only when required by law, educational authorities, CBSE regulations, or government agencies.

7. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to understand visitor behavior, improve website performance, and provide a better user experience.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. Maharishi Vidya Mandir is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

9. Changes to This Policy

The school reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page and become effective immediately upon publication.

10. Contact Information

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or personal information, please contact Maharishi Vidya Mandir School.
